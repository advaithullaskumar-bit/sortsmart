Track 3 — Product Build

SortSmart ♻️

Vision-Powered Waste Segregation & Safety Intelligence Core

Hackathon: Be10X Build for Good (2026)
Target User: Urban Housing Societies & Sanitation Staff
AI Model: Google Gemini 3.7 / 3.6 Multimodal Vision
GitHub Repo: github.com/advaithullaskumar-bit/sortsmart

1. What Real Problem Are You Solving?

In urban housing societies and municipal wards, up to 40% of recyclable and compostable waste batches are rejected at processing centers due to cross-contamination (e.g., wet food waste ruining cardboard, or hazardous batteries mixed with dry plastic packaging).

  • Pain Point: Residents face hesitation categorizing ambiguous items (e.g., greasy pizza boxes, e-waste cables, unlabelled batteries).
  • Cost & Safety Impact: Facility managers spend 2 to 3 hours daily manually re-sorting contaminated bins, exposing sanitation workers to toxic/hazardous materials.

2. Who Is The Problem For?

Primary Users: Housing society residents, Resident Welfare Association (RWA) committees, and municipal sanitation staff in urban Indian housing complexes.

Testing Context: Evaluated with real household waste scenarios covering 4 primary waste streams: Wet Waste, Dry Waste, E-Waste, and Hazardous Household Waste.

3. How Does Your Solution Use AI?

SortSmart combines multimodal computer vision with a safety-first intelligence engine:

  • Multimodal Vision Perception: Uses Google Gemini Vision API (gemini-3.7-flash) to analyze raw photos of waste items in real-time, extracting object type, material composition, and condition.
  • Safety-First Intelligence Core: Cross-checks perception against strict policy rules:
    • Refuses Low Confidence: Items perceived with < 72% confidence are automatically routed to a Human Review Required queue rather than making an unsafe guess.
    • Safety Over Convenience: Items flagged as hazardous (batteries, needles, chemicals) are strictly routed to Hazardous Waste, overriding convenience classifications.
  • Why Non-AI Falls Short: Rule-based forms require users to already know department names and technical material classifications. Keyword search cannot process unstructured photos of dirty, damaged, or mixed waste items.

4. System Architecture & Flow

[ User Uploads Photo ] │ ▼ [ Gemini 3.7 Vision API ] ──► Parses Image & Computes Perception Confidence │ ▼ [ SortSmart Intelligence Core ] ├── Confidence < 72%? ────► Route to "Human Review Required" ├── Hazardous Material? ────► Force "Hazardous Waste" Stream (Safety Override) └── Clear Match? ────► Assign Bin (Wet / Dry / E-Waste) & Provide Disposal Guide │ ▼ [ Interactive UI Dashboard ] ──► Displays Bin, Reason, Contamination Risk & Leaderboard
🎯 Measurable Impact:
SortSmart compresses a 15-minute search for municipal disposal guidelines into a 3-second camera scan, eliminating batch cross-contamination for an entire housing society.
